Doubles IQ

Fifteen live points. One question each time:
where should you be?

How it works

  1. A point plays out. The ball stops at the moment of decision.
  2. Press the court where you — the gold player — should move to, then slide to fine-tune. Guide lines show the exact spot past your fingertip.
  3. Lock it in. You get the coach's answer, the reasoning, and a score.

All three levels are open from the start — start wherever you like, and tap any ball on the results screen to study that spot again.

You are always the gold player at the bottom of the screen. Your partner is blue, opponents are red.

Deuce and ad are mirrored. Your deuce court is the right half of your side — but the opponents' deuce court is the left half of theirs, diagonally across. That is why a deuce-court serve travels diagonally. The four corners of the court are labelled so you never have to work it out.

Built for phones. Add it to your home screen and drill between matches.
